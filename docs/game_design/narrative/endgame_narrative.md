# Endgame Narrative

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Finale choice consequences, epilogue-state categories, postgame continuity, faction response, and narrative state after the main campaign

## 1. Purpose

The ending resolves the immediate Second Cascade while preserving a playable postgame.

## 2. Final Choice

The canonical final choices are:

- Stabilize;
- Sever;
- Contain.

All three prevent the immediate catastrophic cascade.

## 3. Stabilize

### Action

The player uses distributed safeguards to maintain controlled anomaly-assisted route operation.

### Required Narrative Capability

Stabilize requires the strongest combination of:
- research;
- Continuance understanding;
- coalition support;
- functional Reach IV infrastructure.

### Consequences

- more advanced routes remain available;
- factions debate control/governance;
- Asterion strongly supports continued study;
- Helios support depends on safeguards/trust;
- regional trade potential increases.

## 4. Sever

### Action

The player permanently disables the anomaly-linked Transit Lattice architecture in the playable region.

### Consequences

- immediate technical risk is minimized;
- selected Reach IV/anomaly shortcuts close;
- conventional routes remain;
- Freehold/local autonomy benefits;
- Asterion loses major research opportunity;
- Continuance nodes adjust to reduced containment burden.

## 5. Contain

### Action

The player isolates the active mechanism without restoring it or destroying it.

### Consequences

- anomaly-linked routes remain restricted;
- the site becomes a guarded research/containment zone;
- Asterion/Continuance cooperation can deepen;
- future risk remains manageable but unresolved.

## 6. Coalition Quality

The ending epilogue also evaluates:

- faction reputation;
- major faction arcs;
- restored routes;
- surviving settlements;
- rescued specialists;
- station development;
- evidence disclosure choices.

## 7. No Single Best Score

The game does not reduce the ending to one Good Ending score.

Different outcomes emphasize:
- connectivity;
- safety;
- autonomy;
- knowledge.

## 8. Character Epilogues

Major character epilogues depend on relevant state.

Examples:
- Mara Venn: research future;
- Tarek Vale: Helios security policy;
- Nia Calder: Freehold reconstruction/recruitment state;
- Oren Kade: industrial corridor future;
- Sable Rook: Blackwake status;
- Custodian Seven: Continuance directive state.

## 9. Home Station

The home station remains owned and playable after the ending.

Its built state is not replaced by a cinematic canonical version.

## 10. Postgame Routes

World updates according to final choice.

GDS-12/GDS-7 route-state implementation must ensure:
- no required home-station access is lost;
- postgame remains navigable;
- closed routes are clearly shown.

## 11. Remaining Content

After the finale the player can continue:
- faction missions;
- exploration;
- station building;
- ship construction;
- raids;
- research;
- survivor rescue;
- endgame activities.

## 12. Narrative Completion

The main Fracture mystery is sufficiently resolved at the ending.

Optional mysteries about Ancient Structures can remain for postgame/future content without making the base story feel incomplete.

## 13. Save State

The finale creates a persistent **Postgame Resolution State**.

It does not force New Game+ or reset progression.

## 14. New Game+

New Game+ is not an initial requirement.

It can be added later without changing the canonical ending logic.

## 15. Explicit Non-Goals

The ending does not:
- destroy the player's station by default;
- reset the save;
- reveal one secret perfect option;
- invalidate faction choices;
- require permanent crew death.

## 16. Dependencies

This specification depends on Main Story, Factions, Reputation, World Routes, Missions, Progression, and Persistence.

## 17. Open Questions

None in the ending-state baseline.
