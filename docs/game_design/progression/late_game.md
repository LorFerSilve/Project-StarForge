# Late Game — P3/P4

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** P3 Strategic Power and P4 Verge Expedition progression, advanced-system maturity, Reach III/IV transition, raid/endurance escalation, and finale-preparation prerequisites

## 1. Scope

Late Game covers:

- **P3 — Strategic Power / Reach III**;
- **P4 — Verge Expedition / Reach IV**.

Primary geography includes:

- Kestrel;
- Lyra;
- Nacre;
- Nadir;
- Pale Crown;
- Eidolon;
- Threshold;
- Null Choir;
- the Crownless approach.

## 2. Late-Game Purpose

Late Game tests whether the player's interconnected operation can support complex expeditions rather than merely whether individual equipment numbers are high enough.

The player must combine:

- advanced Research;
- specialized industry;
- robust station support;
- Reach III/IV spacecraft;
- prepared robot forces;
- faction/intelligence relationships;
- raid capability;
- anomaly/Ancient-system understanding;
- recovery capacity.

## 3. P3 Entry

P3 begins only after the P2 Exit Gate is satisfied.

Entering P3 does not grant advanced technology automatically.

It means the world/campaign has reached the point where Reach III operations and the Act III evidence chain are legitimately accessible.

## 4. P3 Strategic Identity

P3 is the first phase in which Horizon can function as a **strategic stronghold** rather than merely an industrial home base.

The player is expected to make meaningful choices about:

- redundancy;
- high-power systems;
- defensive depth;
- specialist staffing;
- robot attrition/replacement;
- long-range expedition support;
- faction consequences;
- high-value raids.

## 5. Station Maturity

P3 should make **S3 — Fortified Research/Logistics Station** achievable and begin the path toward **S4 — Strategic Nexus**.

Typical capabilities:

- redundant power and life-support paths;
- mature manufacturing/repair capability;
- advanced Research laboratories;
- defended storage and strategic-item security;
- external shields/weapons;
- internal security/lockdown;
- robot production/service;
- robust communications/sensors;
- ship refit capability appropriate to advanced hulls.

No stage label substitutes for these physical systems.

## 6. Advanced Energy

P3 is the normal maturation band for:

- Nuclear Handling where still relevant;
- Superconductive Materials;
- Fusion Fuel Handling;
- Fusion Engineering;
- high-capacity shields;
- high-end thermal management.

Fusion is a capability chain, not one abstract upgrade.

## 7. Advanced Robotics

P3 is the normal access band for:

- Breaching Unit;
- Demolition Unit;
- Heavy Mech;
- Advanced Robotics;
- Command Link III/IV;
- Heavy Robot Bay;
- mature tactical squad composition.

Ownership remains optional unless a mission's real physical requirements demand a capability that only an available robot/tool/support path can provide.

## 8. High-End Spacecraft

P3 should support:

- advanced Corvette configurations;
- specialized Utility/Scout builds;
- Frigate construction/acquisition where legitimately unlocked;
- Reach III propulsion;
- heavier power/thermal/shield/weapon systems;
- longer endurance;
- expanded robot/cargo support.

Frigate remains an option, not a mandatory linear replacement.

## 9. Serious Raid Capability

P3 is the intended band for multi-phase raids where the player is expected to understand:

- target intelligence;
- external defense suppression;
- boarding/breaching;
- local system sabotage;
- reinforcement interruption;
- physical looting;
- extraction under pressure.

Targets remain world-authored/generated threats and do not automatically scale to player gear.

## 10. P3 Economy

The economy now supports strategic procurement rather than only convenience.

High-value transactions can include:

- advanced components;
- shipyard/service work;
- rare blueprint/data access;
- intelligence;
- high-end ammunition;
- strategic resource contracts.

Finite stock, access, liquidity, and physical transport still apply.

## 11. P3 Resource Focus

Common resource acquisition should be substantially less manual if the player invested in automation/trade.

The main player-facing scarcity shifts toward:

- advanced circuits;
- superconductors;
- fusion inputs;
- platinum/tungsten/titanium demand;
- Voltaic Crystal;
- Exotic Matter Sample;
- Ancient technology;
- unique Research Evidence/Blueprints.

## 12. P3 Narrative Spine

The canonical Act III progression includes:

- MS-A301 — Rook's Wake;
- MS-A302 — Sealed Authority;
- MS-A303 — The Lattice Equation.

The exact mission/story authority remains GDS-7/GDS-8/GDS-11.

## 13. Lattice Equation Gate

MS-A303 requires the three authoritative evidence packages defined by the story mission contract.

The player cannot substitute Credits, generic Research time, or unrelated faction reputation for missing mandatory archives.

## 14. P3 Exit / P4 Entry

P3 transitions into P4 when all of the following are true:

1. **MS-A303 — The Lattice Equation has committed Success**;
2. Fracture Verge coordinates and the legitimate Reach IV Research path are known;
3. the required Reach IV Research/Blueprint/component chain is available to pursue;
4. the player can legitimately commission or acquire an operational Reach IV spacecraft configuration;
5. the Eidolon staging path is available under world/story access rules.

No generic level threshold applies.

## 15. Reach IV Is Not Free

P4 does not begin merely because the player knows Crownless exists.

The player still needs actual:

- Research;
- Blueprints/design access;
- advanced components;
- station/vendor fabrication/refit capability;
- compatible ship hull/configuration;
- propulsion/navigation;
- fuel/propellant;
- operational condition.

## 16. P4 Identity

P4 changes the late game from expansion into **expedition readiness under extreme uncertainty**.

The player is now preparing to operate beyond ordinary post-Fracture infrastructure.

Primary concerns become:

- Reach IV reliability;
- endurance;
- redundancy;
- anomaly survival;
- repair/recovery;
- advanced navigation;
- Ancient-system interaction;
- finale intelligence.

## 17. P4 Spacecraft Readiness

An operational Reach IV configuration is mandatory for the core Verge campaign.

The qualifying ship must satisfy all applicable GDS-6 fitting and mission requirements.

A Frigate is not mandatory.

A smaller hull can qualify only if it genuinely fits the required:

- Reach IV drive/navigation;
- power;
- thermal rejection;
- life support;
- cargo/support;
- repair/endurance;
- mission utility.

## 18. P4 Player Readiness

Normal Verge operations require the player to have legitimate capability for:

- sealed environmental protection/EVA where mission requires;
- sufficient Life-Support Reserve;
- engineering interaction/repair;
- scanning/anomaly investigation;
- combat where hostile blockers exist;
- carrying/securing required mission objects.

A mission validator checks actual equipment/capability rather than a player level.

## 19. P4 Robot Readiness

Robots remain a major strategic option in P4.

A player preparing for Crownless should normally have access to:

- hardened communications;
- useful repair/support capability;
- combat/security capability;
- sufficient TCC for a meaningful squad;
- valid transport/service.

Exact finale minimums are owned by `endgame.md`.

## 20. P4 Station Readiness

Horizon must remain capable of supporting the final expedition loop.

The minimum late-game station functions that must have a recoverable operational path are:

- Command Core/control;
- communications;
- ship service/refit;
- strategic storage;
- required Research/analysis;
- fabrication/repair;
- stable life support for resident crew.

The station need not be visually maximal or own every optional module.

## 21. P4 Narrative Spine

The canonical P4 progression includes:

- MS-A401 — Beyond the Map;
- MS-A402 — Verge Entry;
- MS-A403 — The Older Signal;
- MS-A404 — Wake Pattern;
- MS-F01 — Coalition preparation.

## 22. MS-A401 Validation

MS-A401 is the first explicit campaign validation of Reach IV expedition capability.

If the ship is not valid, the mission reports real blockers and remains pending.

It does not fail the campaign or consume a fictional Reach token.

## 23. Threshold Staging

MS-A402 establishes a persistent safe staging solution at Threshold.

The player cannot skip this by possessing a stronger ship if the route-reference objective remains required.

## 24. Ancient Systems

MS-A403 connects late-game progression to Ancient Systems evidence and the Crownless relation.

Ancient/Anomaly progression cannot be bought through a generic high Credit price when the required discovery is unique.

## 25. Second Cascade Crisis

MS-A404 commits the Second Cascade crisis state and unlocks finale preparation.

This event does not itself make the finale deployable.

MS-F01 must still validate the complete Finale Readiness Gate.

## 26. Late-Game Dynamic Events

Late-game Dynamic Events may become more strategically consequential through world state:

- faction conflict;
- high-value convoys;
- advanced distress/recovery;
- anomaly events;
- severe but paced Horizon attacks;
- strategic shortages;
- fortified target opportunities.

They still obey event budgets, Recovery Grace, finite sources, and no offline progression.

## 27. No Difficulty-Based Unlocks

All P3/P4 story, Reach, Research, and finale routes remain available on all Difficulty profiles.

Difficulty cannot require additional grind to compensate for easier combat.

## 28. Recovery Before Finale

The player may delay MS-F01/MS-F02 to repair Horizon, rebuild robots, earn Credits, gather resources, or strengthen faction support.

There is no real-world countdown that permanently ends the campaign merely because the player takes active-game time to prepare, unless an explicit authored story state introduces a bounded timer.

The baseline finale preparation has no hidden irreversible countdown.

## 29. Anti-Grind

The late game must not require repeating starter content for bulk resources if the player has developed viable industrial/trade systems.

Late-game effort should primarily target meaningful strategic bottlenecks.

## 30. Optional Mastery

A highly optimized P4 operation may include:

- S4-level Horizon capability;
- Frigate;
- maximum normal external TCC;
- deep faction alliances;
- redundant ship systems;
- broad Research completion;
- extensive Credit reserves;
- optimized defense networks.

None is automatically a finale hard gate unless `endgame.md` explicitly states it.

## 31. Explicit Non-Goals

Late Game does not require:

- every faction allied;
- every Research project complete;
- every robot class deployed;
- Frigate specifically;
- maximum Credits;
- maximum crew skills;
- all optional raids;
- a Gear Score.

## 32. Tuneable Parameters

Advanced recipe costs, event pacing, market availability, required expedition reserves, Research Work, threat values, repair burden, and optional-content rewards are tuneable.

The P3/P4 phase/gate relationships are fixed.

## 33. Dependencies

Depends on Progression Model, all domain progression specs, GDS-7/GDS-8 story execution, GDS-11 Raids, Economy, Dynamic Events, Difficulty, Failure/Recovery, and Endgame.

## 34. Open Questions

None in the P3/P4 late-game progression contract.
