## Authority Boundary

This domain owns:

- the P0–P5 campaign capability-phase model;
- cross-domain advancement relationships;
- phase entry/exit contracts;
- player/station/ship/crew/robot/research/galaxy progression pacing;
- early-, mid-, late-, and endgame readiness expectations;
- final MS-F01 readiness validation and P5 postgame continuation.

It does not redefine the internal mechanics of the systems being progressed. Those remain authoritative in GDS-2 through GDS-11 and the GDS-12 cross-cutting system specifications.

## Core Baseline

Progression is capability-based rather than level-based.

Canonical phases:

- **P0 — Recovery / Recommissioning**;
- **P1 — Local Operator / Reach I**;
- **P2 — Frontier Network / Reach II**;
- **P3 — Strategic Power / Reach III**;
- **P4 — Verge Expedition / Reach IV**;
- **P5 — Second Cascade Resolution / Postgame**.

These phases summarize real state and never grant free capability.

The first-pass design establishes:

- no generic Player Level, Gear Score, Base Level, Robot Level, Ship Level, or prestige ladder;
- station maturity through actual infrastructure rather than a Town Hall abstraction;
- actual Strategic Reach plus route knowledge/verification for galaxy access;
- bounded Tactical Control Capacity progression;
- Research pacing without automatic phase unlocks;
- automation of solved low-value repetition as the campaign advances;
- explicit P1, P2, P3, and P4 progression transitions;
- a concrete MS-F01 finale-readiness gate;
- no mandatory Frigate finale requirement;
- finale robot participation without requiring maximum TCC or one specific robot class;
- four finale Support Channels with faction/Continuance or self-sufficient provider paths;
- persistent postgame after Stabilize, Sever, or Contain rather than forced reset/New Game+.

## Finale Readiness Baseline

MS-F01 validates actual:

- mandatory story state;
- Operational Reach IV spacecraft;
- departure reserve manifest;
- player environmental/tool/combat loadout;
- Horizon strategic support state;
- at least 6 base Field TCC;
- Tactical Squad Command Load of at least 4;
- combat/security plus relevant robot utility capability;
- Navigation, Engineering/Recovery, Combat/Security, and Analysis/Containment Support Channels.

No single human faction is a mandatory finale dependency.
