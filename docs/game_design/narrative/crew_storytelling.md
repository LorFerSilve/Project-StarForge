# Crew Storytelling

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** Narrative use of recruited crew, background hooks, authored crew conversations, personal objectives, station reactions, and procedural-story limits

## 1. Purpose

Crew should feel like people inhabiting the station without requiring a full social simulator.

## 2. Crew Narrative Layers

Crew storytelling uses:

- background;
- profession;
- traits;
- origin/faction;
- rescue context;
- station assignment;
- selected personal conversations;
- authored personal objectives for notable crew.

## 3. Procedural Crew

Procedural recruits can receive generated background hooks.

Examples:
- former mine technician;
- colony medic;
- cargo mechanic;
- displaced researcher.

These hooks can influence flavor dialogue but do not generate unlimited bespoke questlines.

## 4. Authored Crew

Selected characters such as Nia Calder can have:

- fixed background;
- personal arc;
- unique dialogue;
- specific recruitment condition.

After recruitment they still use normal crew mechanics.

## 5. Station Reactions

Crew can react to major station/world events such as:

- new sector reached;
- serious attack;
- Fracture discovery;
- faction alliance;
- major station expansion.

Reactions are contextual flavor unless explicitly tied to a persistent choice.

## 6. Personal Objectives

A notable crew member can request an optional objective.

Completion can affect:
- dialogue;
- trait/background resolution;
- special knowledge;
- reputation;
- narrative state.

It does not grant arbitrary unexplained stat bonuses.

## 7. No Mandatory Morale System

Crew storytelling does not introduce a hidden morale/happiness meter.

## 8. No Romance Requirement

Romance is not part of the baseline authoritative scope.

## 9. Crew Conflict

Characters can disagree narratively.

Routine disagreement does not make crew randomly abandon the station unless an authored departure system is later specified.

## 10. Injury Dialogue

Crew can acknowledge:
- injury;
- recovery;
- player rescue;
- station disaster.

This does not alter health rules.

## 11. Assignment Awareness

Dialogue can reference current profession/assignment when plausible.

Example:
- engineer comments on overloaded reactor;
- farmer comments on crop shortages.

## 12. Crew Knowledge

Crew dialogue uses knowledge available to:
- that crew member;
- station records;
- player-shared discoveries.

## 13. Narrative Character Recruitment

When a major narrative character becomes recruitable, their persistent Story Character ID maps to exactly one Crew identity.

No duplicate narrative/crew actor exists.

## 14. Save/Load

Conversation completion, personal objective state, and authored relationship flags persist.

## 15. Death Boundary

Routine systemic injury does not permanently kill crew.

If a future authored narrative death exists, this document must explicitly identify the character/event before implementation.

No baseline GDS-7 story mission requires recruited crew permadeath.

## 16. Explicit Non-Goals

Crew storytelling does not require:
- full relationship simulation;
- romance;
- happiness bars;
- daily personal quests for every crew member;
- random abandonment.

## 17. Dependencies

This specification depends on GDS-3 Crew, Major Characters, Dialogue, Factions, Missions, and Presentation.

## 18. Open Questions

None in the crew-story baseline.
