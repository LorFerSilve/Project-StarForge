# Mid Game — P2

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** P2 Frontier Network progression, capability maturation, Reach II economy/faction integration, systemic raid introduction, and P3 entry conditions

## 1. Scope

Mid Game covers **P2 — Frontier Network / Reach II**.

Primary geography:

- Vesper;
- Bastion;
- Kara;
- Orison;
- other Inner Frontier locations made available by the canonical route graph.

## 2. Mid-Game Purpose

P2 is the transition from a recovering local operator into a self-directed interstellar organization.

The defining change is not a level increase. It is the simultaneous maturation of:

- Horizon Station industry;
- specialist crew coverage;
- repeatable trade;
- faction relationships;
- Reach II spacecraft operation;
- tactical robots;
- Research specialization;
- credible defensive preparation.

## 3. Entry Contract

P2 begins when the P1 campaign exit commits and the player has a legitimate Reach II progression path.

A P2 player may still have unfinished Recovery Sector content.

Entering P2 does not automatically grant:

- a Reach II ship;
- faction reputation;
- advanced robots;
- advanced Research;
- Credits;
- blueprints.

## 4. Intended Player Role

The player is no longer expected to perform every solved station task personally.

Normal play increasingly alternates between:

- strategic preparation at Horizon;
- faction/world interaction;
- specialized expeditions;
- industrial planning;
- targeted research;
- ship/robot configuration;
- selected direct maintenance during exceptional failures.

## 5. Horizon Station Maturity

P2 should make **S2 — Automated Industrial Hub** practically achievable.

Typical station capability includes:

- stable power with meaningful reserve/redundancy;
- stable atmosphere/water/food support;
- functioning refining and manufacturing chains;
- useful storage/logistics automation;
- first sustained Research operation;
- robot fabrication/service;
- expanded docking/service;
- basic external defense and security response.

S2 is descriptive capability state only. The player receives no free unlock merely for matching the stage.

## 6. Crew Maturity

P2 should support a multi-specialist crew rather than one universal operator.

Expected staffing pressure includes:

- Engineering;
- Logistics;
- Agriculture/food support;
- Research;
- Medicine;
- Robotics/Mechanics;
- Navigation/Intelligence or Security as the organization expands.

No one named recruit is mandatory unless an authored story mission explicitly requires that character.

## 7. Crew Progression

P2 is the first phase in which sustained crew skill progression, training, mentorship, and workload allocation should materially alter station efficiency.

The system must preserve one Primary Assignment and finite Workload Capacity.

## 8. Economy Maturity

P2 introduces economy as a strategic alternative rather than a tutorial convenience.

The player should be able to:

- buy scarce inputs;
- sell legitimate surplus;
- compare regional prices;
- purchase services;
- acquire eligible blueprints/data/intel;
- operate trade-oriented cargo configurations.

Trade remains constrained by:

- market stock;
- vendor liquidity;
- faction access;
- physical cargo;
- route access;
- travel cost/risk.

## 9. No Mandatory Merchant Build

The player is not required to become a trader.

A viable P2 campaign can obtain required progression through combinations of:

- missions;
- mining;
- salvage;
- Research;
- faction rewards;
- trade.

Critical campaign capability must have at least one non-arbitrage acquisition path.

## 10. Faction Expansion

P2 establishes meaningful contact with multiple major human institutions and the Continuance path.

The player may improve or damage individual relationships independently.

No single human faction may become the only permanent route to the main campaign.

## 11. Research Maturity

P2 moves Research beyond foundational survival technology into specialization such as:

- Advanced Metallurgy;
- Nuclear Handling;
- Superconductive Materials;
- Advanced Shields;
- Advanced Robotics foundations;
- improved propulsion/navigation;
- anomaly-analysis prerequisites.

Actual unlocks still require their authoritative Evidence, project, Blueprint, lab, staffing, and material conditions.

## 12. Industry Maturity

P2 should make multi-stage production strategically relevant.

The player begins deciding whether to:

- mine raw inputs;
- buy raw/refined inputs;
- fabricate components internally;
- purchase finished components;
- salvage intact equipment;
- reserve strategic materials for future projects.

The economy cannot make internal industry irrelevant by providing infinite cheap finished goods.

## 13. Spacecraft Maturity

Reach II is the normal operating band.

Expected viable hull progression includes:

- improved Scout;
- Utility Cutter;
- early Corvette where obtained.

The player should encounter real tradeoffs between:

- cargo;
- combat capability;
- robot transport;
- sensors;
- endurance;
- power/thermal margin.

## 14. Robot Maturity

P2 makes a tactical robot squad a normal optional capability rather than a novelty.

The intended band includes:

- Assault Unit;
- Hacker Unit;
- Shield Unit;
- existing Recon/Repair roles;
- Command Link II and later Link III availability;
- meaningful ship Robot Rack support.

The player may own a reserve fleet larger than active TCC.

## 15. Raid Introduction

P2 is the normal introduction band for **standard fortified raids** after foundational mission, extraction, ship, combat, and robot mechanics are understood.

Initial raids should emphasize readable systemic opportunities:

- reconnaissance;
- one or two meaningful defense dependencies;
- physical breach/access;
- limited sabotage;
- physical loot extraction.

## 16. Horizon Defense Maturity

Procedural hostile attacks can now become a normal system if their Dynamic Event prerequisites are satisfied.

Attack generation must consider current station vulnerability and Recovery Grace.

The player is expected to have had a reasonable opportunity to construct detection and response capability before severe attack profiles enter the candidate pool.

## 17. Dynamic Events

P2 broadens the event pool to include:

- faction requests/conflicts;
- trade shortages/surpluses;
- distress calls;
- salvage opportunities;
- route incidents;
- moderate Horizon defense events;
- research/anomaly opportunities.

Dynamic Events supplement the authored campaign; they do not replace mandatory story missions.

## 18. Resource Pressure

Common resources should increasingly be obtainable through multiple repeatable channels.

P2 scarcity should shift toward:

- advanced materials;
- specialist components;
- Research Evidence;
- blueprints;
- faction access;
- strategic fuel/ship components.

This prevents the mid game from being dominated by repeated starter ore runs.

## 19. Failure Pressure

P2 failures may create material setbacks through:

- robot destruction;
- lost Field-Unsecured loot;
- ship/station repair bills;
- consumed ammunition/fuel;
- lost economic opportunity;
- reputation changes.

The game still does not erase committed Knowledge Assets or core campaign state through ordinary failure.

## 20. P2 Main-Story Spine

The canonical P2 story progression is centered on:

- MS-A201 — The Institute;
- MS-A202 — Security Line;
- MS-A203 — Custodian Seven;
- MS-A204 — Echo Laboratory.

Their exact mission behavior remains GDS-8/GDS-7 authority.

## 21. P2 Exit Gate

P2 transitions to P3 only when all of the following are true:

1. **MS-A204 — Echo Laboratory has committed Success**;
2. the campaign has exposed the legitimate Reach III technology/navigation path;
3. the player owns or can commission an **operational Reach III-capable spacecraft configuration**;
4. at least one required Reach III route on the canonical campaign path is known and eligible for verification/use;
5. Horizon Station remains capable of supporting the player's continuing expedition loop or has an explicit recoverable path if temporarily damaged.

There is no Player Level, Credit total, generic Gear Score, or total mission-count requirement.

## 22. Reach III Commissioning Boundary

Requirement 3 is satisfied only by actual ship configuration validation.

A story flag does not substitute for:

- propulsion;
- navigation;
- power;
- thermal support;
- fuel/propellant;
- operational condition.

## 23. Softlock Protection

If the player damages one faction relationship, mandatory Reach III progression must remain achievable through another authored path involving Research, alternate access, recovery, or another faction.

The game may make the route harder or more expensive, but not permanently impossible through ordinary reputation play.

## 24. Optional Mid-Game Mastery

Before leaving P2, a highly developed player may also possess:

- mature automated farming/logistics;
- several trained specialists;
- diversified trade relationships;
- an advanced Corvette;
- a large robot reserve;
- redundant Horizon defenses;
- several optional faction blueprints.

These are advantages, not universal hard gates.

## 25. Difficulty

Difficulty changes pressure within its allowed GDS-12 axes but does not change P2 entry/exit gates, faction availability, Research requirements, Reach, or reward entitlement.

## 26. Anti-Grind Rule

No P2 mandatory gate may require an arbitrary number of repeated generic missions solely to fill a progression bar.

Required resources/evidence can demand effort, but their acquisition must correspond to an actual capability chain.

## 27. Explicit Non-Goals

P2 does not require:

- Frigate ownership;
- Heavy Mech ownership;
- full faction alliance;
- maximum crew skills;
- all Research branches;
- all Horizon modules;
- Reach IV;
- a generic level threshold.

## 28. Tuneable Parameters

Mission reward quantities, market availability, Research Work, fabrication time, station operating margins, enemy pressure, Dynamic Event pacing, and exact material costs are tuneable.

The P2 capability and exit structure is fixed.

## 29. Dependencies

Depends on Progression Model, Station/Crew/Spacecraft/Robot/Research/Economy progression, GDS-7 story/world, GDS-8 Missions, GDS-11 Raids, Dynamic Events, Difficulty, and Failure/Recovery.

## 30. Open Questions

None in the P2 mid-game progression contract.
