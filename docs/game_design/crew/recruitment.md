# Crew Recruitment

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Conditions, flow, acceptance, station capacity checks, and conversion of eligible survivors into persistent crew

## 1. Purpose

Recruitment converts a rescued or otherwise eligible character into a persistent station crew member.

Recruitment is a deliberate player decision.

## 2. Recruitment Eligibility

A character can be recruited only if:

- their encounter/class permits recruitment;
- required rescue or story conditions are satisfied;
- hostile status has been resolved where applicable;
- recruitment is not blocked by an authored faction/narrative condition.

## 3. Recruitment Flow

The standard flow is:

Encounter → Rescue or Meet → Learn Relevant Information → Reach Safe Interaction State → Offer Recruitment → Capacity Check → Character Acceptance → Station Onboarding → Crew Roster.

## 4. Player Choice

The player is never forced to permanently recruit every rescued survivor.

Possible outcomes are:

- recruit;
- decline;
- defer where allowed;
- direct the survivor to another safe outcome when authored.

## 5. Character Acceptance

Eligible characters can have authored acceptance conditions.

Examples:

- sufficient faction reputation;
- completed rescue;
- completion of a personal objective;
- safe station capability.

Procedural recruits normally accept once rescued and a sustainable station slot is available, unless their generated background defines a clear condition.

## 6. No Recruitment Currency Fee by Default

Recruitment itself does not require paying a generic recruit token or premium-style currency.

A survivor may have contextual needs or story conditions, but joining is not a gacha purchase.

## 7. Station Capacity Check

Permanent recruitment requires sustainable station support.

The check includes relevant:

- habitation capacity;
- atmosphere;
- food projection;
- water projection;
- safety.

The interface shows the limiting category.

## 8. Soft vs Hard Capacity

Physical bed/habitation capacity is a hard onboarding requirement for permanent crew.

Food and water are operational sustainability warnings rather than absolute menu locks if current stored reserves can support onboarding.

The player is warned before recruiting into projected shortage.

## 9. Emergency Guest State

A rescued survivor who cannot yet be permanently onboarded can remain a Temporary Guest if emergency guest capacity exists.

Guests:

- consume basic support;
- do not count as assigned crew;
- cannot hold permanent jobs;
- can later be recruited after capacity expands.

## 10. Onboarding

On recruitment, the game creates the persistent crew member state and assigns:

- identity;
- profession;
- skills;
- traits;
- unassigned duty status;
- habitation allocation.

No automatic job assignment occurs unless the player enabled a valid auto-assignment policy.

## 11. Initial Assignment

The player is prompted to:

- inspect skills;
- assign a primary role;
- choose a work zone/facility if relevant.

The player may leave a new recruit Unassigned.

## 12. Unassigned Crew

Unassigned crew still consumes station support and remains present.

They do not provide full professional automation merely by being on the station.

## 13. Duplicate Recruitment Prevention

A persistent character cannot be recruited more than once.

Save/load and repeated encounter generation must preserve identity.

## 14. Dismissal

The baseline does not treat crew dismissal as instant deletion.

If later supported, departure must have an explicit safe destination and persistence rule.

Until that system is specified, permanent recruited crew remains part of the roster.

## 15. Faction Consequences

Recruiting certain authored characters can affect faction relations where explicitly defined.

Generic recruitment does not secretly modify diplomacy.

## 16. Recruitment UI

The recruitment screen shows:

- identity;
- profession;
- known skills;
- known traits;
- special knowledge;
- current health;
- station-capacity impact;
- any acceptance condition.

## 17. Edge Cases

If capacity disappears between offer and onboarding, onboarding pauses and explains the cause.

If the recruit is injured, recruitment can complete while they enter medical/recovery state.

If the station is under active attack, onboarding waits until a safe state.

## 18. Explicit Non-Goals

Recruitment does not use loot boxes, premium currency, randomized paid pulls, or abstract card packs.

## 19. Dependencies

This specification depends on survivor encounters, station crew support, assignments, injuries, factions, narrative, and persistence.

## 20. Open Questions

None in the current baseline.
