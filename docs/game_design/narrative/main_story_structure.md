# Main Story Structure

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Canonical campaign acts, story-gate logic, main mystery progression, mandatory discoveries, and high-level story outcomes

## 1. Purpose

The main story provides a structured long-term arc through the same progression systems used by the wider game.

## 2. Campaign Structure

The baseline campaign has:

- Prologue;
- Act I;
- Act II;
- Act III;
- Act IV;
- Finale;
- Postgame.

## 3. Prologue — The Silent Forge

### Goal

Recover the dormant home station.

### Required Narrative Beats

1. Player detects emergency/transponder signal.
2. Player approaches/boards the damaged station.
3. Minimum power is restored.
4. Command Core becomes accessible.
5. Station emergency intelligence SERA activates.
6. Player receives Acting Recovery Administrator status.
7. Station logs reveal it belonged to a pre-Fracture recovery/industrial network.
8. Nearby distress/route data creates the first external objectives.

### Progression Result

- home station acquired;
- starting fabrication/life support available;
- nearby Reach I locations revealed.

## 4. Act I — Signals in the Dark

### Theme

Re-establish local survival and contact.

### Required Beats

- restore a local Deep-Space Relay;
- rescue at least one survivor;
- establish first sustainable station operations;
- make first contact with Freehold and/or Meridian presence;
- encounter evidence that the local relay failed during The Fracture for reasons beyond simple damage;
- recover first Transit Lattice telemetry fragment.

### Story Discovery

The local collapse followed a wider synchronized event.

### Progression Gate

Player obtains enough technology/information for reliable Reach II travel.

## 5. Act II — Broken Maps

### Theme

The factions remember different versions of history.

### Required Beats

- enter Inner Frontier;
- contact Asterion;
- encounter Helios route-security authority;
- encounter a Continuance node;
- recover scientific evidence from a pre-Fracture research site;
- learn that Transit Lattice anomaly prediction and autonomous control were tightly coupled.

### Major Choice Layer

Player determines which faction receives copies of sensitive intermediate evidence.

This affects reputation/access but does not erase the underlying evidence from player knowledge.

### Story Discovery

The Fracture involved both anomalous physics and emergency control actions.

### Progression Gate

Reach III propulsion/research path becomes available.

## 6. Act III — Contested Truth

### Theme

Knowledge becomes strategically dangerous.

### Required Evidence Set

The player must assemble three independent evidence packages:

1. **Lattice Telemetry Archive** — what the network physically experienced;
2. **Containment Directive Archive** — what autonomous systems were ordered to do;
3. **Anomaly Model Archive** — what researchers believed would happen.

### Sources

The exact order can vary.

Sources involve:

- Asterion archive;
- Helios sealed records;
- Continuance node;
- Blackwake-held stolen archive;
- abandoned pre-Fracture installation.

### Faction Conflict

Factions disagree over whether evidence should be:

- published;
- controlled;
- weaponized;
- destroyed;
- used to restore routes.

### Story Discovery

No single actor caused the event; attempts to contain it amplified a system-wide cascade.

### Progression Gate

The player identifies the Fracture Verge and the requirements for Reach IV.

## 7. Act IV — The Verge Protocol

### Theme

Understanding the past is not enough; the mechanism is becoming active again.

### Required Beats

- construct/obtain Reach IV-capable ship;
- enter Fracture Verge;
- investigate Ancient Structures;
- authenticate or defeat containment barriers;
- discover active anomaly growth;
- establish that dormant Transit-Lattice remnants are synchronizing again.

### Major Strategic Choice

The player builds a response coalition.

Support can come from factions based on:

- reputation;
- completed faction arcs;
- preserved technology;
- rescued specialists;
- world-state outcomes.

No single faction is mandatory if equivalent capability can be assembled another way.

## 8. Finale — Second Cascade

### Crisis

A Fracture-like synchronization event begins.

### Player Objective

Reach and stabilize the primary Verge control/anomaly site before the cascade propagates.

### Required Systems

The finale intentionally draws on:

- ship propulsion;
- station industry;
- research;
- faction relationships;
- crew;
- combat;
- robots;
- navigation;
- raids/boarding;
- resource preparation.

### Final Decision

The player chooses one of three strategic resolutions:

1. **Stabilize** — preserve controlled high-reach route technology under distributed safeguards;
2. **Sever** — permanently disable the dangerous anomaly-linked route architecture in the playable region;
3. **Contain** — isolate the system and preserve it for future research without active use.

All three stop the immediate cascade.

## 9. Ending Consequences

### Stabilize

- strongest restored connectivity;
- highest future opportunity;
- requires strongest coalition/research;
- factions debate governance.

### Sever

- safest immediate technical outcome;
- some advanced routes close;
- regional independence increases.

### Contain

- routes remain limited;
- dangerous technology preserved;
- Asterion/Continuance relationships strongly influence epilogue.

## 10. No Fake Secret Perfect Ending

There is no hidden fourth ending that makes all tradeoffs disappear.

A highly prepared player can improve collateral outcomes but not erase the core strategic choice.

## 11. Campaign Flexibility

Acts II–IV allow optional faction arcs and differing mission order.

Mandatory evidence can be acquired through more than one faction path where practical.

## 12. Main Story Lock Protection

Low reputation with one human faction cannot permanently make the campaign impossible.

Alternative sources/reconciliation paths exist for mandatory evidence.

## 13. Optional Story Content

Optional arcs can cover:

- faction internal conflicts;
- unique survivors;
- pre-Fracture facilities;
- Continuance nodes;
- Blackwake clans;
- Ancient Structures.

## 14. Postgame

After the finale:

- home station remains playable;
- remaining missions/faction content continues where world state permits;
- endgame activities adapt to the chosen resolution;
- no mandatory save reset occurs.

## 15. Explicit Non-Goals

The main story does not require:

- one faction-exclusive campaign;
- universal happy ending;
- chosen-one reveal;
- single villain boss as sole explanation;
- permanent campaign failure from routine mission defeat.

## 16. Dependencies

This specification depends on Lore, Factions, Reputation, Story Missions, Spacecraft Reach, Research, Missions, Combat, Robots, Raids, and Progression.

## 17. Open Questions

None in the campaign-act baseline.

Exact mission encounter mechanics are owned by GDS-8/GDS-9/GDS-11.
