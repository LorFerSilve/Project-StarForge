# Crew Progression

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** Cross-phase crew roster/profession/skill growth, specialist availability expectations, training progression, redundancy goals, and campaign integration

## 1. Existing Authority

GDS-3 remains authoritative for Crew skills, XP, professions, assignments, training, traits, Workload Capacity, injuries, and no routine permadeath.

This document defines how those systems mature across the campaign.

## 2. No Crew Level

Crew does not gain a generic level or rarity tier.

Long-term value comes from:

- domain-specific Skill 0–5;
- profession;
- traits;
- Special Knowledge;
- training;
- assignment history;
- team composition.

## 3. Progression Goal

Crew progression should transform Horizon from player-dependent operation into a supervised organization with specialist redundancy and reliable automation.

## 4. P0

The player begins with little or no specialist coverage.

Early station recovery is intentionally hands-on.

Survivors encountered during recovery become the first meaningful staffing decisions.

## 5. P1

Expected priorities:

- Engineer/Mechanic coverage;
- Farmer or agriculture competence;
- basic Medic support;
- initial Logistics/Robotics capability where found;
- basic workload assignment.

The player should not need every profession immediately.

## 6. P2

Horizon should begin supporting dedicated specialists in:

- Engineering;
- Farming;
- Science;
- Medicine;
- Logistics;
- Robotics;
- Security/Weapons as threat rises.

Cross-training starts reducing single-person dependencies.

## 7. P3

The roster should support:

- multiple departments simultaneously;
- expert-level specialist growth;
- mentoring/training;
- redundant engineering/medical/security coverage;
- advanced Robotics/Navigation/Intelligence roles;
- raid/defense supervision.

## 8. P4

Final-expedition preparation expects sufficient expertise somewhere in the player's organization or coalition to support:

- Reach IV navigation/ship operation;
- advanced engineering/repair;
- anomaly/Ancient analysis;
- robot/defense support;
- medical recovery.

No one exact Crew ID is mandatory unless a story mission explicitly requires that character.

## 9. Specialist Redundancy

Late progression should reward avoiding a station where one Incapacitated specialist disables an entire critical domain.

Redundancy can come from:

- second trained crew member;
- cross-trained specialist;
- automation;
- robots;
- faction service/support.

## 10. Workload Remains Finite

Higher skill improves capability/throughput but does not turn one Master into infinite station staffing.

## 11. Skill XP

Skill-specific XP remains earned through meaningful work/training.

Campaign phase does not award free skill levels.

## 12. Training Infrastructure

As Horizon develops, training can improve through:

- simulator/training spaces;
- mentors;
- Research knowledge;
- faction instruction;
- specialized certification.

## 13. Special Knowledge

Late systems may require Special Knowledge such as Fusion Reactor Certification, Ancient Navigation Protocols, or Advanced Shield Calibration.

The requirement must name the knowledge/capability rather than demand a generic crew level.

## 14. Recruitment

Crew expansion comes from actual survivor/contact/recruitment interactions.

Phase advancement does not auto-spawn employees.

## 15. Economy

Some training/services/recruitment arrangements can involve Credits, but Credits cannot purchase instant Skill 5 mastery without the authored training/time requirement.

## 16. Automation

Crew progression and automation are complementary:

- specialist defines/supervises policy;
- robots/machinery execute bounded routine work;
- improved systems reduce repetitive manual burden.

## 17. Injury

Injury can temporarily remove specialist capacity.

Skill/knowledge is not lost because the crew member is injured.

## 18. No Skill Decay

Campaign advancement/time does not decay skills.

## 19. Difficulty

Difficulty does not change crew XP rewards, maximum skill, or profession rules.

## 20. Postgame

Postgame crew growth focuses on:

- mastering remaining skills;
- mentoring;
- cross-training;
- completing specialist knowledge;
- optimizing department coverage.

There is no infinite prestige.

## 21. Explicit Non-Goals

No crew rarity, global crew level, free phase-based skill grants, offline training, or mandatory maxing every profession.

## 22. Tuneable Parameters

XP thresholds, training speed, mentorship efficiency, roster growth cadence, and specialist task throughput remain tuneable.

## 23. Dependencies

Depends on GDS-3 Crew, Station Progression, Research Progression, Dynamic Events/Survivor missions, Economy, and Progression Model.

## 24. Open Questions

None in the cross-phase crew progression baseline.
