# Spacecraft Progression

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Cross-phase player spacecraft access, hull-class role progression, Reach capability bands, upgrade philosophy, service dependencies, and finale ship readiness

## 1. Purpose

Spacecraft progression expands what the player can reach, carry, survive, fight, and support without turning ships into a single linear tier ladder.

## 2. Existing Hull Authority

GDS-6 remains authoritative for the baseline player hull classes:

- Shuttle;
- Scout;
- Utility Cutter;
- Corvette;
- Frigate.

GDS-12 defines their intended progression bands and access relationships, not their flight/damage rules.

## 3. No Ship Level

Ships do not gain generic levels or experience points.

Capability comes from:

- hull variant;
- installed modules;
- power/thermal capacity;
- propulsion;
- sensors;
- cargo;
- weapons/shields/armor;
- utility/robot support;
- condition.

## 4. Hull Classes Are Not Strict Replacements

Larger hulls are not universally better.

Small ships can remain useful through:

- lower mass;
- lower fuel/propellant demand;
- agility;
- lower operating cost;
- access to smaller docking interfaces;
- specialist scouting roles.

## 5. P0

The player begins with access to a damaged or low-capability Shuttle/Scout-class craft sufficient for local recovery operations.

It supports Reach I only after required minimum repairs/configuration.

## 6. P1 — Reach I

Expected viable hulls:

- Shuttle;
- Scout.

Typical progression:

- improved cargo;
- sensors;
- life support;
- basic shields/armor/weapons;
- reliable Reach I drive;
- first robot rack where fitting permits.

## 7. P2 — Reach II

Expected access expands to:

- improved Scout variants;
- Utility Cutter;
- early Corvette access.

Typical capabilities:

- stronger power/thermal systems;
- improved docking/service;
- dedicated robot transport;
- advanced sensors;
- stronger defensive/combat options;
- Reach II propulsion/navigation.

## 8. P3 — Reach III

Expected viable fleet options:

- specialized Scout/Utility builds;
- advanced Corvette;
- Frigate project/access begins where station infrastructure/Blueprints permit.

Typical capabilities:

- fusion-class energy/propulsion;
- heavier weapons/armor/shields;
- expanded cargo/robot support;
- longer expedition endurance;
- Reach III.

## 9. P4 — Reach IV

Final expedition requires an **operational Reach IV-capable spacecraft configuration**.

It does **not** require the Frigate class specifically.

A smaller hull may qualify if it legitimately satisfies all:

- Reach IV propulsion/navigation;
- power/thermal requirements;
- expedition life support;
- required cargo/support;
- required access/mission utility;
- survival/repair constraints.

## 10. Frigate Role

Frigate is the largest required baseline player hull class and a late-game strategic option.

It offers high:

- module capacity;
- robot support;
- cargo;
- combat endurance;
- redundancy.

Its costs/handling keep smaller ships relevant.

## 11. No Capital-Ship Requirement

Player-owned capital ships are outside the baseline progression requirement.

The finale and postgame are fully completable with the defined hull classes.

## 12. Reach Progression

Strategic Reach remains GDS-6/GDS-7 authority:

- Reach I — Recovery Sector;
- Reach II — Inner Frontier routes;
- Reach III — Contested Reach / Outer Expanse routes;
- Reach IV — Fracture Verge.

A Reach class is a real propulsion/navigation capability, not a story flag alone.

## 13. Research Dependency

Higher Reach and advanced ship modules require relevant:

- Research;
- Blueprint;
- materials/components;
- fitting compatibility;
- station/vendor service.

## 14. Station Service Dependency

Large/advanced refits can require Horizon/faction facilities with:

- compatible dock/hangar;
- heavy handling;
- power;
- manufacturing/repair tools;
- required staff/automation.

Credits alone cannot bypass physical service capability.

## 15. Robot Support Progression

Ship robot support progresses through:

- Robot Rack access;
- additional Rack Units;
- charging/data interfaces;
- Heavy Robot Bay;
- command/sensor support module.

Transport capacity remains separate from TCC.

## 16. Ship Command Support

A compatible operational **Command Support Module** can add **+2 TCC** to the player's Personal TCC during external field deployment.

Only the single strongest valid ship/platform support contribution applies; multiple ship modules do not stack TCC bonuses.

Normal external field TCC is capped at **10** before authored finale exceptions.

## 17. Cargo Progression

Larger/better cargo configurations enable:

- bulk mining;
- raid loot recovery;
- trade;
- robot transport;
- expedition supplies.

Cargo remains constrained by Mass and Volume.

## 18. Combat Progression

Ship combat capability grows through real:

- weapon mounts;
- ammunition;
- shields;
- armor;
- power;
- thermal systems;
- sensors.

There is no hidden ship-combat-rating multiplier.

## 19. Failure

Ship damage can temporarily remove Reach/combat/utility capability.

The established Primary Ship remains a recoverable long-term asset under GDS-6 rather than being routine-deleted.

## 20. Economy

Ships/modules/services can be purchased where market/faction access permits, but trade cannot bypass Research/Blueprint/fitting/service requirements.

## 21. Difficulty

Difficulty does not change Reach requirements, fuel logic, fitting constraints, or unlock bands.

## 22. Postgame

Postgame ship progression emphasizes:

- alternate hull variants;
- optimized specializations;
- fleet redundancy;
- high-end modules;
- completion of faction technology.

No endless Ship Level is added.

## 23. Explicit Non-Goals

No ship XP level, mandatory Frigate finale, capital-ship requirement, automatic obsolescence of small hulls, or Reach unlock purely from campaign phase.

## 24. Tuneable Parameters

Module costs, operating cost, cargo capacity, TCC support value/cap, fitting values, and unlock pacing are tuneable.

## 25. Dependencies

Depends on GDS-6 Spacecraft, GDS-4 Research/Blueprints, GDS-7 routes, GDS-10 Robots/TCC, Station Progression, Economy, and Progression Model.

## 26. Open Questions

None in the spacecraft progression baseline.
