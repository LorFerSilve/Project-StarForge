# Story Missions

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Canonical main-story mission IDs, narrative objectives, required discoveries, ordering constraints, and story progression outputs

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what the main-story missions must accomplish narratively.

GDS-8 later defines their exact gameplay objective mechanics, encounter structures, failure rules, and reward quantities.

## 2. Mission Chain Philosophy

The story uses a partially branching chain.

Some missions can be completed in flexible order, but mandatory discoveries and act gates are fixed.

## 3. Prologue

### MS-P01 — Silent Signal

Location:
- Aster's Rest System — Horizon Station

Narrative Goal:
- introduce player;
- discover dormant home station.

Required Beats:
- detect emergency transponder;
- approach station;
- establish first physical entry;
- discover that some internal systems still have emergency power.

Output:
- station registered as active recovery target.

### MS-P02 — Heart of the Station

Location:
- Aster's Rest System — Horizon Station

Narrative Goal:
- claim/recover home station.

Required Beats:
- restore minimum power;
- restore Command Core;
- activate SERA;
- accept Acting Recovery Administrator status;
- restore minimal safe habitation/air.

Output:
- home station ownership;
- SERA contact;
- first route/relay coordinates.

## 4. Act I — Signals in the Dark

### MS-A101 — Broken Relay

Location:
- Caldera System — Relay ECHO-12

Goal:
- restore local Deep-Space Relay.

Required Discovery:
- relay's failure logs indicate synchronized errors from outside the local system.

Output:
- expanded Recovery Sector map;
- first Transit Lattice telemetry fragment.

### MS-A102 — The Living

Location:
- Morrow System — Morrow Rescue Corridor / associated mission site

Goal:
- resolve a survivor distress site.

Required Beat:
- demonstrate survivor rescue/transport loop.

Output:
- first survivor/recruit opportunity;
- Freehold contact lead.

### MS-A103 — A Line Reopened

Location:
- Recovery Sector route network centered on Morrow ↔ Aster's Rest/Caldera

Goal:
- establish a stable local corridor and first regular outside contact.

Output:
- Freehold/Meridian access;
- Reach II progression path becomes discoverable.

## 6. Act III — Contested Truth

Act III centers on acquiring three evidence packages.

### Evidence Package A — Lattice Telemetry Archive

Can be obtained through an authored Helios/archive path or dangerous abandoned facility path.

### Evidence Package B — Containment Directive Archive

Can be obtained from Continuance systems or stolen Blackwake records derived from a sealed facility.

### Evidence Package C — Anomaly Model Archive

Obtained through Asterion-linked scientific investigation and physical anomaly evidence.

### MS-A301 — Rook's Wake

Location:
- Kestrel System — Rook's Wake

Goal:
- confront/negotiate/raid Captain Sable Rook's network for stolen archive intelligence.

Possible Narrative Outcomes:
- hostile recovery;
- negotiated exchange;
- conditional cooperation.

Output:
- archive source or location data.

### MS-A302 — Sealed Authority

Location:
- Lyra System — Authority Archive L-4

Goal:
- obtain suppressed pre-Fracture emergency records.

Output:
- institutional evidence;
- Helios/Asterion political consequences.

### MS-A303 — The Lattice Equation

Location:
- assembled at Horizon Station after required evidence is secured

Goal:
- combine the three evidence packages.

Required Discovery:
- The Fracture was a cascading interaction between anomaly physics, network coupling, emergency control actions, and autonomous containment.

Output:
- Fracture Verge coordinates;
- Reach IV research requirement;
- Act IV unlocked.

## 8. Finale

### MS-F01 — Coalition

Location:
- Horizon Station with remote/physical faction support

Goal:
- assemble sufficient capability to respond.

Inputs can include:

- faction support;
- station industry;
- crew specialists;
- robots;
- ships;
- research;
- resources.

The exact required capability model is defined by GDS-12/GDS-8.

### MS-F02 — Second Cascade

Location:
- Crownless System — Crownless Lattice Nexus

Goal:
- reach final Fracture control/anomaly site;
- overcome external/internal defenses;
- stabilize the site;
- choose final resolution.

Final Choice:
- Stabilize;
- Sever;
- Contain.

Output:
- campaign ending;
- persistent postgame world state.

## 9. Failure and Retry

Main-story missions can fail tactically.

Failure does not permanently delete the mission chain.

Retry/recovery rules belong to GDS-8.

## 10. Reputation Branches

Different faction relationships can alter:

- briefing contact;
- access method;
- supporting units;
- side objectives;
- dialogue;
- rewards.

Mandatory evidence remains obtainable through at least one valid route.

## 11. Player Knowledge State

Each mandatory discovery is a persistent Knowledge/Narrative flag.

Replaying content cannot duplicate Research Evidence or story unlocks.

## 12. Mission Unlock Visibility

The game communicates why a story mission is blocked:

- Reach;
- research;
- faction access;
- prior evidence;
- ship capability.

No invisible act gating.

## 13. Explicit Non-Goals

Story missions do not require:
- one exact faction route;
- unrepeatable campaign-ending failure;
- hidden mandatory grinding;
- narrative rewards outside established systems.

## 14. Dependencies

This specification depends on Main Story, Factions, Reputation, GDS-8 Missions, GDS-6 Reach/Navigation, GDS-4 Research, Combat, Robots, and Raids.

## 15. Open Questions

None in narrative mission ordering/outputs.

Encounter-level mechanics remain GDS-8 authority.
