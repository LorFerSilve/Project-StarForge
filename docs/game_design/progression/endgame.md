# Endgame — MS-F01, MS-F02, and P5

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Finale readiness validation, coalition/support commitments, MS-F01 capability gate, MS-F02 deployment prerequisites, ending transition, and P5 postgame progression

## 1. Purpose

Endgame defines the exact cross-domain readiness conditions for the Second Cascade finale and the persistent postgame state after Stabilize, Sever, or Contain.

It does not redefine the narrative meaning of the three endings or the mission execution owned by GDS-7/GDS-8/GDS-11.

## 2. Endgame Sequence

Canonical sequence:

MS-A404 — Wake Pattern Success  
→ MS-F01 — Coalition / Readiness  
→ Finale Readiness Gate satisfied  
→ MS-F02 — Second Cascade deployable  
→ Stabilize / Sever / Contain commits  
→ P5 Postgame Resolution State.

## 3. MS-F01 Is a Readiness Mission

MS-F01 has no ordinary tactical fail state.

If readiness is insufficient:

- the mission remains active/pending;
- missing requirements are listed explicitly;
- the player may leave preparation and continue other gameplay;
- no resources are consumed merely for failing validation.

## 4. No Generic Finale Level

The finale cannot require:

- Player Level;
- Gear Score;
- Base Level;
- Credit total;
- total mission count;
- percentage completion.

It validates real capability.

## 5. Finale Readiness Gate — Mandatory Story State

The following story/world conditions are mandatory:

1. MS-A404 — Wake Pattern has committed Success;
2. Second Cascade crisis state is active;
3. Crownless Lattice Nexus location/route information is known;
4. all mandatory unique evidence/knowledge required by the final story path has already been committed;
5. MS-F02 has not already reached a committed terminal resolution state.

## 6. Finale Readiness Gate — Spacecraft

The selected finale ship must be a persistent player-controlled spacecraft in Operational condition and must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Strategic Reach IV** propulsion/navigation capability;
2. valid Crownless route compatibility;
3. sufficient operational power for the installed expedition configuration;
4. sufficient thermal rejection for the installed expedition configuration;
5. functioning navigation and sensors required by the route/mission;
6. functioning life support for all carried occupants for the mission's declared endurance envelope;
7. valid cargo capacity for mandatory mission equipment and reserved supplies;
8. valid docking/EVA/access capability required by the authored MS-F02 route;
9. enough fuel/propellant to satisfy the mission's departure reserve manifest;
10. no unresolved damage state that invalidates any mandatory capability above.

A Frigate is **not** required.

## 7. Finale Departure Reserve Manifest

MS-F02 owns a data-defined departure manifest containing minimum quantities/capacity for:

- required fuel;
- required propellant;
- life-support reserve;
- mandatory mission consumables;
- required repair/spare resources;
- required mission-specific equipment/cargo.

The exact quantities are tuneable content data.

The fixed rule is that validation uses actual owned/loaded resources and reserves them at deployment commit; no abstract readiness percentage substitutes for them.

## 8. Finale Readiness Gate — Player Equipment

The player must have a valid deployed loadout providing:

- sealed environmental protection appropriate to MS-F02 EVA/interior hazards;
- functioning helmet/suit seal;
- sufficient Life-Support Reserve or compatible refill path;
- Engineering Multitool or equivalent required repair/interface capability;
- scanner/anomaly-interaction capability required by the final control path;
- at least one functioning combat-capable weapon with compatible ammunition/energy;
- inventory Mass/Volume headroom for mandatory portable objective equipment where required.

The mission validator checks capability tags, not one specific item model.

## 9. Finale Readiness Gate — Horizon Station

Before departure, Horizon must have an operational or explicitly redundant path for:

- Command Core/control;
- long-range communications;
- required final Research/analysis data access;
- ship departure/service interface;
- strategic data persistence.

Noncritical damaged station modules do not block the finale.

The player is not required to repair every cosmetic or optional fault.

## 10. Finale Readiness Gate — Robot Command Capacity

The selected expedition must have **at least 6 available Field TCC before temporary coalition support**.

This requires legitimate player/platform capability under Robot Progression rules.

Finale coalition support may add +2 temporary TCC, subject to the finale external cap of 12.

## 11. Finale Readiness Gate — Robot Force

The player must load at least one valid Tactical Squad whose combined Command Load is **4 or greater**.

The squad must contain:

- at least one unit capable of direct combat/security support; and
- at least one unit capable of at least one utility role relevant to MS-F02: Repair, Hacking, Breaching, Shield support, Recon, or equivalent authored robot capability.

One robot may satisfy both categories only if its actual configuration legitimately provides both capabilities.

All deployed robots must satisfy normal transport, energy, ammunition, environment, condition, and TCC rules.

## 12. Why Robots Are a Finale Requirement

Robots are one of Project StarForge's primary progression pillars.

MS-F02 is the capstone systemic expedition and therefore requires the player to demonstrate a modest but real tactical-robot capability.

The requirement is intentionally below maximum TCC and does not mandate Heavy Mech or any one robot class.

## 13. Finale Support Channels

MS-F01 organizes readiness into four **Support Channels**:

1. Navigation and Route Support;
2. Engineering and Recovery Support;
3. Combat and Security Support;
4. Analysis and Containment Support.

Every channel must have one valid provider before departure readiness can commit.

## 14. Support Provider Types

A channel can be satisfied by:

- an allied/faction commitment;
- a Continuance/node commitment where narratively valid;
- Horizon/player self-sufficient capability explicitly recognized by that channel.

The game does not require alliance with every faction.

## 15. Navigation and Route Support

This channel is satisfied by either:

### External Provider
An explicit faction/Continuance navigation-support commitment valid for MS-F02;

**or**

### Self-Sufficient Provider
All of:
- verified Crownless route solution;
- operational Reach IV ship navigation;
- advanced sensor/navigation package recognized by the mission;
- required route-reference data secured.

## 16. Engineering and Recovery Support

Satisfied by either:

### External Provider
An explicit repair/recovery/logistics support commitment;

**or**

### Self-Sufficient Provider
All of:
- functioning ship field-repair capability;
- required spare/repair resources in the departure manifest;
- at least one deployed Repair-capable robot or an equivalent authored repair support capability;
- valid recovery plan/extraction asset defined by MS-F02.

## 17. Combat and Security Support

Satisfied by either:

### External Provider
An explicit escort/security/fire-support commitment;

**or**

### Self-Sufficient Provider
All of:
- selected ship has at least one functioning defensive or combat capability applicable to the finale route;
- player loadout satisfies the combat requirement;
- deployed robot squad satisfies Section 11.

## 18. Analysis and Containment Support

Satisfied by either:

### External Provider
An explicit Asterion/Continuance/other valid analysis-containment commitment;

**or**

### Self-Sufficient Provider
All of:
- required final Research projects/knowledge are completed;
- Horizon has operational analysis/data capability at departure;
- ship/player carries the mission-defined anomaly/control interface capability;
- required Quantum/Ancient-system hardware for the selected authored path is installed/carried where specified.

## 19. No Single-Faction Softlock

Every Support Channel has at least one non-exclusive provider route.

A hostile Helios, Meridian, Asterion, Freehold, or Blackwake relationship can remove that faction's support option but cannot permanently block the finale if the player satisfies an alternate provider or self-sufficient path.

## 20. Support Commitments Are Not Free Items

A faction commitment can provide authored:

- escort force;
- route data;
- repair support;
- analysis capability;
- temporary TCC support;
- extraction/recovery assistance.

It does not duplicate the player's cargo/resources or silently upgrade ship stats.

## 21. MS-F01 Success Commit

MS-F01 succeeds only when:

1. all mandatory story-state conditions pass;
2. selected ship passes finale validation;
3. selected player loadout passes validation;
4. Horizon minimum support state passes;
5. robot TCC and Tactical Squad minimums pass;
6. all four Support Channels have valid providers;
7. the departure manifest is satisfiable from actual available resources;
8. the player explicitly commits Departure Readiness.

## 22. Departure Readiness Commit

On commit:

- selected ship is registered as finale expedition ship;
- selected robot squad/transport assignments are registered;
- support providers are locked for the current deployment attempt;
- required mission cargo/resources are reserved for loading/deployment;
- MS-F02 becomes Deployable.

The commit does not yet consume fuel/ammunition merely for opening the mission screen.

## 23. Readiness Invalidated Before Deployment

If, after MS-F01 readiness commit but before MS-F02 deployment, a mandatory capability becomes invalid because the player:

- removes a required module;
- spends reserved resources through an explicit override;
- damages the ship;
- unloads required robots;
- loses a support provider through a committed story state;

MS-F02 returns to **Blocked — Readiness Invalidated** and lists the failed gate.

MS-F01 does not need to rerun narratively; readiness can be revalidated.

## 24. Atomic Deployment Validation

Immediately before MS-F02 deployment commits, the complete readiness validator runs again.

Deployment is atomic with:

- mission instance creation/activation;
- resource reservation/consumption states defined by travel;
- robot/ship/player deployment ownership;
- support commitment activation.

Save/load cannot duplicate the departure manifest or support commitment.

## 25. MS-F02 Failure

Ordinary tactical failure follows GDS-5/GDS-6/GDS-8/GDS-10/GDS-11 and `failure_recovery_rules.md`.

It does not permanently erase the finale.

The player may need to:

- recover/repair the ship;
- rebuild destroyed robots;
- replace consumed resources;
- revalidate support/readiness;
- redeploy.

## 26. Finale Retry and Unique State

Unique story evidence, Crownless location knowledge, and already committed main-story discoveries cannot duplicate or disappear through retry.

Mission-specific world damage is handled by its persistent/retry contract.

## 27. Final Decision

MS-F02 requires exactly one final resolution:

- Stabilize;
- Sever;
- Contain.

The final selection is a committed narrative transaction.

There is no hidden fourth optimal ending.

## 28. Final Decision Save Boundary

The game must create a Stable Save Boundary immediately **before** the irreversible final-choice confirmation when technically safe.

After the player confirms one resolution and the commit completes, ordinary subsequent autosaves/manual saves persist that chosen Postgame Resolution State.

Loading an older user-selected manual save from before the choice remains permitted; loading a newer save cannot make the committed choice internally uncommitted.

## 29. P5 Entry

P5 begins when MS-F02 completes and one Postgame Resolution State commits.

The campaign does not reset.

## 30. Postgame Persistence

P5 preserves, subject to the chosen ending's authored world consequences:

- Horizon Station;
- player equipment;
- Credits;
- crew;
- ships;
- robots;
- Research;
- Blueprints;
- Evidence;
- faction reputation;
- unresolved compatible missions;
- persistent raid targets;
- market/world state;
- discovered routes still physically/narratively valid.

## 31. Ending Consequences

Stabilize, Sever, and Contain may alter:

- route availability;
- faction attitudes;
- Dynamic Event weighting;
- Continuance behavior;
- anomaly activity;
- epilogue missions;
- selected market/logistics conditions.

They do not erase unrelated earned progression without an explicitly authored consequence.

## 32. Postgame Activities

P5 supports compatible continuation through:

- unfinished side missions;
- exploration;
- trade;
- mining/salvage;
- research completion;
- station optimization;
- ship/fleet optimization;
- robot fleet specialization;
- faction content;
- raids;
- dynamic events;
- authored post-resolution missions.

## 33. No Forced New Game+

The baseline does not require New Game+, prestige, seasonal reset, or campaign wipe after the ending.

A future New Game+ mode would require a separate accepted specification.

## 34. No Infinite Vertical Scaling

Postgame does not introduce endless enemy/player stat escalation.

Long-term mastery comes from:

- specialization;
- alternate technology;
- optimized infrastructure;
- fleet redundancy;
- optional difficult fixed-world content;
- system efficiency;
- completing remaining knowledge/faction paths.

## 35. Postgame Difficulty

Difficulty remains the chosen profile and may be changed according to `difficulty_and_scaling.md`.

The ending and postgame rewards are not difficulty-exclusive.

## 36. Postgame Economy

Markets continue where their locations/routes remain valid after the ending.

Ending-driven shortages, route closures/openings, and faction policy can change local economic state through normal Economy/Dynamic Event rules.

## 37. Postgame Raids

Persistent raid targets retain their state.

Compatible factions may continue offensive/defensive events based on reputation, recovery, world state, and the chosen ending.

No target resets merely because credits rolled.

## 38. Finale Failure Burden

Repeated finale attempts must remain consequential through actual consumed resources, robot attrition, ship/equipment damage, and recovery time.

There is no arbitrary escalating retry fee.

## 39. Finale Anti-Grind

If the player once satisfied a knowledge/story gate, retry cannot require re-farming that already committed Knowledge Asset.

Only genuinely consumed/lost physical capability must be restored.

## 40. Accessibility / Presentation Dependency

GDS-13 must clearly present finale readiness as named capability checks and distinguish:

- hard blocker;
- warning;
- optional improvement;
- support-provider choice;
- resource reserve requirement.

It must not summarize readiness only as one opaque percentage.

## 41. Explicit Non-Goals

Endgame does not require:

- Frigate specifically;
- every faction allied;
- every Research project;
- every robot class;
- maximum TCC;
- maximum Credits;
- all side missions;
- difficulty-exclusive ending;
- permanent campaign reset;
- endless prestige scaling.

## 42. Tuneable Parameters

Departure-manifest quantities, support-force sizes, finale TCC temporary support, individual encounter pressure, repair burden, and postgame event weights are tuneable.

The readiness categories, 6 base Field TCC minimum, Tactical Squad Command Load >=4, four-channel support requirement, and persistent postgame structure are fixed first-pass rules.

## 43. Dependencies

Depends on GDS-7 Narrative/Factions, GDS-8 Story Mission Execution, GDS-10 Robots/TCC, GDS-11 Raids, all GDS-12 cross-cutting systems, and all progression-domain specifications.

## 44. Open Questions

None in the baseline finale-readiness and postgame contract.
