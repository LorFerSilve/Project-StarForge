# Major Characters

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** Canonical major recurring characters, faction roles, baseline motivations, recruitability, and story function

## 1. Purpose

Major characters provide recognizable human/machine perspectives on the world without replacing systemic crew generation.

## 2. SERA

### Full Designation

**SERA — Station Emergency Recovery Architecture**

### Role

Degraded operations intelligence embedded in the home station.

### Function

- activates during Prologue;
- explains surviving station authority;
- provides technical context;
- records station restoration;
- surfaces old station archives.

### Personality

- precise;
- restrained;
- initially procedural;
- gradually develops broader contextual reasoning as systems are restored.

### Limitation

SERA is not omniscient and has incomplete/corrupted pre-Fracture archives.

### Recruitability

Not a crew member; fixed station system.

## 3. Dr. Mara Venn

### Faction

Asterion Institute.

### Role

Senior anomaly physicist.

### Motivation

Understand The Fracture well enough to prevent repetition.

### Strength

Technically rigorous and willing to challenge faction narratives.

### Flaw

Can underestimate political/operational risk when evidence is scientifically valuable.

### Story Function

Primary scientific interpreter of anomaly evidence.

### Recruitability

Not baseline recruitable; remains faction contact.

## 4. Marshal Tarek Vale

### Faction

Helios Directorate.

### Role

Regional route-security commander.

### Motivation

Prevent another uncontrolled technological catastrophe.

### Strength

Disciplined, strategically competent, protective of civilian routes.

### Flaw

Defaults toward control, classification, and military restriction.

### Story Function

Represents security-first response to Fracture technology.

### Recruitability

Not recruitable.

## 5. Nia Calder

### Faction

Freehold Coalition.

### Role

Frontier systems engineer and settlement recovery specialist.

### Motivation

Keep isolated communities self-sufficient and politically independent.

### Strength

Practical engineering and crisis response.

### Flaw

Distrusts centralized institutions even when coordination would help.

### Story Function

Introduces Freehold perspective and practical survivor network.

### Recruitability

Yes, after completion of her authored Freehold recovery arc.

If recruited, she uses normal Crew mechanics as an Engineer with authored background/traits.

## 6. Oren Kade

### Faction

Meridian Compact.

### Role

Regional logistics director.

### Motivation

Restore profitable, stable industrial corridors.

### Strength

Understands supply chains and resource constraints better than most political leaders.

### Flaw

Can justify aggressive control of infrastructure as "necessary efficiency."

### Story Function

Industrial access, shipyard/resource politics, and Meridian perspective.

### Recruitability

Not recruitable.

## 7. Captain Sable Rook

### Faction

Blackwake Corsairs.

### Role

Commander of the Blackwake crew **Rook's Wake**.

### Motivation

Remain independent, profitable, and impossible for major factions to control.

### Strength

Excellent raider, navigator, and intelligence broker.

### Flaw

Treats trust as a transaction until repeatedly proven otherwise.

### Story Function

Provides pirate perspective and one path to the stolen Containment/Lattice archives.

### Recruitability

Not recruitable.

### Relationship

Can become:

- enemy;
- temporary ally;
- uneasy neutral contact.

## 8. Custodian Seven

### Faction

The Continuance.

### Designation

**Continuance Node CUSTODIAN-7**

### Role

Ancient infrastructure-management node controlling a sealed Transit Lattice facility.

### Motivation

Execute a damaged combination of preservation and containment directives.

### Personality

Not humanized into ordinary emotion.

Communication is formal, conditional, and directive-driven.

### Story Function

Provides Continuance records and demonstrates that some machine nodes prevented greater disaster.

### Recruitability

Not recruitable.

## 9. The Player

The player is a major character but remains customizable according to Player Role.

## 10. Character Conflict Matrix

### Mara Venn vs Tarek Vale

Knowledge vs containment.

### Tarek Vale vs Nia Calder

Central authority vs local autonomy.

### Oren Kade vs Nia Calder

Industrial efficiency vs community independence.

### Sable Rook vs all institutional factions

Anti-authority opportunism.

### Custodian Seven vs everyone

Directive compatibility rather than political loyalty.

## 11. Character Survival

The baseline main campaign does not require unavoidable permanent deaths of these characters.

Specific optional outcomes can remove access/relationship, but routine mission failure does not randomly delete them.

## 12. Character Knowledge

Each character has bounded knowledge.

No character supplies the complete Fracture truth alone.

## 13. Repeated Dialogue

Major characters should update dialogue based on:

- current act;
- reputation;
- major discoveries;
- faction decisions;
- finale preparation.

## 14. Crew Integration

Nia Calder, if recruited, follows:

- crew assignment;
- health;
- skill;
- persistence;
- no routine permadeath.

Her narrative identity does not exempt her from general crew rules.

## 15. Explicit Non-Goals

Major characters do not exist only as exposition terminals, nor does every faction need a romanceable representative.

## 16. Dependencies

This specification depends on Factions, Reputation, Main Story, Dialogue, Crew, and Missions.

## 17. Open Questions

None in the baseline recurring-character roster.

Additional local characters can be authored later without changing these core roles.
