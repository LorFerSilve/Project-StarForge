# Canonical Region Map

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** Fixed baseline named sectors, systems, major story locations, faction hubs, and strategic routes required by the base campaign

## 1. Purpose

This document prevents later mission design from inventing the campaign's geography.

Procedural side locations may be added around this fixed backbone.

## 8. Unlockable Shortcuts

The following optional shortcuts can be restored after relevant exploration:

- Vesper ↔ Kara — restored commercial relay;
- Lyra ↔ Nadir — recovered military lattice route;
- Nacre ↔ Pale Crown — anomaly-assisted route;
- Threshold ↔ Crownless — late-game emergency route after Ancient/Continuance analysis.

A shortcut remains unavailable until its authored unlock condition is met.

## 11. Procedural Expansion

Procedural side locations may be generated inside/around these systems provided they:

- do not remove/rename canonical story locations;
- obey faction/resource/environment profiles;
- do not create alternative mandatory story geography without formal design change.

## 12. Route Persistence

Canonical routes and shortcut unlock states persist.

Story progression cannot silently relocate a canonical node to another system.

## 13. Explicit Non-Goals

The base campaign does not require:
- a procedurally renamed main galaxy;
- random placement of story hubs;
- seamless physical travel between these systems;
- every side system being fixed before implementation.

## 14. Dependencies

This specification depends on Galaxy Structure, Star Systems, Factions, GDS-6 Navigation/Propulsion, Main Story, and GDS-8 Missions.

## 15. Open Questions

None for the canonical campaign geography.

Side-content node counts remain tuneable content data inside this fixed backbone.
