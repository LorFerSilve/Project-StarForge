# Research Progression

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Campaign-band ordering and cross-domain purpose of Research without redefining GDS-4 Research mechanics

## 1. Existing Authority

GDS-4 `resources/research.md` remains authoritative for:

- Research Evidence;
- Technology IDs/projects;
- prerequisite graph;
- Research Work;
- samples;
- Scientist/laboratory requirements;
- no spendable Science Points.

This document defines intended campaign progression bands.

## 2. Research Does Not Equal Phase

Entering a campaign phase does not automatically complete its Research.

The player must still acquire Evidence, operate labs, satisfy prerequisites, and complete Research Work.

## 3. P0 Foundation

Starting/near-start knowledge includes **Basic Fabrication** and the minimum engineering needed to restore Horizon's core systems.

Research focus:

- basic structural/material processing;
- station diagnostics/repair;
- local resource analysis;
- basic navigation/sensors.

## 4. P1 Reach-I Foundation

Intended access band includes:

- Advanced Metallurgy foundation;
- Nuclear Handling where evidence/resources permit;
- basic Robotics;
- improved station life-support/automation;
- early weapon/armor/sensor research;
- propulsion/navigation work required for reliable Reach I operations.

## 5. P2 Reach-II Frontier Research

Core band includes:

- Superconductive Materials;
- Advanced Shields;
- Advanced Robotics foundation;
- improved Electronics/Computing;
- advanced sensors;
- Reach II propulsion/navigation technologies;
- faction-derived industrial/scientific variants.

## 6. P3 Reach-III Strategic Research

Core band includes:

- Fusion Fuel Handling;
- Fusion Engineering;
- mature Advanced Robotics;
- advanced weapon/defense systems;
- Quantum Materials foundation;
- early Quantum Computing where evidence permits;
- Reach III propulsion/navigation;
- advanced anomaly analysis.

## 7. P4 Reach-IV / Fracture Research

Core band includes:

- mature Quantum Computing;
- Ancient Systems;
- Anomaly Physics required for Verge navigation;
- exotic/quantum propulsion/control technologies;
- advanced containment;
- Crownless/Transit Lattice analysis;
- Reach IV enabling technology.

## 8. Evidence Gates

Late Research cannot be brute-forced with time/Credits alone.

Required Evidence must be acquired from actual:

- exploration;
- story archives;
- analysis;
- factions;
- anomalies;
- Ancient/Continuance systems.

## 9. Blueprint Separation

Researching a technology does not automatically provide every finished design.

A Blueprint remains separately required where GDS-4 defines one.

## 10. Early Discovery

The player may discover an advanced Blueprint/Evidence before its intended phase.

If prerequisites are missing, it remains known/owned but unusable/Locked by Technology.

The game does not delete early discoveries solely to enforce phase order.

## 11. Alternate Sources

Where campaign structure allows, a technology can have multiple Evidence/acquisition paths through:

- story mission;
- faction cooperation;
- dangerous exploration;
- recovered archive;
- reverse engineering.

Mandatory progression must not depend on one easily permanently missable procedural event.

## 12. Faction Specialization

Factions provide different Research/Blueprint opportunities consistent with GDS-7:

- Helios: armor, kinetics, defense, military systems;
- Meridian: industry, cargo, mining, ship construction;
- Asterion: sensors, shields, energy, anomaly science;
- Freehold: survival, agriculture, medicine, resilience;
- Blackwake: boarding, improvised weapon/access systems;
- Continuance: robotics/control/legacy infrastructure under node access.

Faction access expands options rather than replacing the canonical Research graph.

## 13. Research Work Pace

Research Work amounts are tuneable.

Mandatory progression should require meaningful projects but not long AFK waiting; all progress is Active Game Time only.

## 14. Research Parallelism

Parallel projects are limited by actual:

- laboratory capacity;
- Scientist Workload;
- equipment;
- power/thermal resources.

Phase advancement does not grant free parallel slots.

## 15. Destructive Samples

Existing GDS-4 destructive/non-destructive analysis rules remain unchanged.

GDS-12 does not refund samples because they are progression-critical; instead mandatory content provides recoverable source paths.

## 16. Research and Economy

Credits can pay for:

- services;
- training;
- physical samples/components;
- Blueprints/data where sold.

Credits cannot buy generic Research Points or automatically complete a technology.

## 17. Research and Difficulty

Difficulty does not modify Research Evidence, project unlocks, or Research Work rewards.

## 18. Research and Failure

Committed completed Research is permanent in the save timeline.

Lab destruction can pause future work but cannot make the station forget completed technology.

## 19. Finale Gate

MS-F01/MS-F02 requires the actual set of Reach IV, Anomaly/Ancient/control-path technologies defined by the final mission's capability checks.

There is no generic Research Level requirement.

## 20. Postgame

Unfinished technologies/Blueprints remain researchable after the finale where ending/world state permits.

No infinite research prestige is required.

## 21. Explicit Non-Goals

No Science Points, Research Level, phase-auto-research, Credits-to-instant-research, difficulty research bonuses, or arbitrary reset.

## 22. Tuneable Parameters

Research Work, lab throughput, sample quantities, source weighting, and timing are tuneable.

## 23. Dependencies

Depends on GDS-4 Research/Blueprints, GDS-7 Narrative/Factions, Galaxy Access Progression, Station/Crew Progression, Economy, and Progression Model.

## 24. Open Questions

None in the research progression baseline.
