# Narrative Cross-Validation

> **Status:** Active Audit  
> **Authority:** GDS-7 narrative consistency review only  
> **Purpose:** Validate player role, main story, major characters, story missions, dialogue, environmental storytelling, crew integration, and endgame against established world/gameplay rules.

## 1. Scope

This audit checks Narrative against:

- Global Game Rules;
- World Lore/Factions/Reputation;
- GDS-2 Home Station;
- GDS-3 Crew;
- GDS-4 Research/Blueprints;
- GDS-5 Player Failure;
- GDS-6 Spacecraft Reach/Navigation.

## 2. Player Identity — Result: PASS

The player is an independent recovery operator and earns strategic importance through play.

No chosen-one, fixed faction, or supernatural origin conflicts with GDS-5 customization/progression.

## 3. Home Station Acquisition — Result: PASS

The Prologue establishes station control through emergency recovery authorization.

Narrative does not alter station construction/system rules.

## 4. Player Failure — Result: PASS

Main-story tactical failure is recoverable/retryable.

Routine defeat does not become canonical player death or erase unrelated long-term progression.

## 5. Crew Permanence — Result: PASS

No baseline main-story mission requires routine permanent recruited-crew death.

Nia Calder, if recruited, maps to one persistent crew identity and obeys GDS-3 rules.

## 6. Research as Story Progression — Result: PASS

Fracture investigation uses:

- Research Evidence;
- archives;
- unique data;
- physical samples;
- technology unlocks.

It does not create a parallel generic story-science currency.

## 7. Blueprint/Technology Boundary — Result: PASS

Narrative rewards may grant:
- research access;
- evidence;
- blueprints;
- route information.

The story does not treat discovering one as automatically granting all others.

## 8. Spacecraft Reach — Result: PASS

Campaign acts align with GDS-6:

- Act I → Reach II path;
- Act II/III → Reach III;
- Act IV → Reach IV.

The story does not bypass propulsion requirements through cutscene travel.

## 9. Faction Reputation — Result: PASS

Story branches can alter independent faction reputation.

No human faction is mandatory as the player's permanent allegiance.

Mandatory story evidence has alternate/reconciliation paths so one low reputation does not brick the campaign.

## 10. Continuance — Result: PASS

Continuance interaction uses authentication/directive state rather than human reputation.

Custodian Seven is a node, not a recruitable human crew member.

## 11. Fracture Truth — Result: PASS

World Lore and Main Story share the same canonical hidden truth:

- anomaly physics;
- Transit Lattice coupling;
- human emergency intervention;
- conflicting autonomous containment;
- institutional suppression.

No single-villain contradiction exists.

## 12. Story Mission Authority — Result: PASS

Narrative Story Missions define:
- mission IDs;
- required story beats;
- required discoveries;
- story outputs.

GDS-8 remains owner of:
- gameplay objective mechanics;
- encounter layout;
- extraction;
- failure implementation;
- reward quantities.

## 13. Dialogue — Result: PASS

Dialogue choices depend on:
- knowledge;
- reputation;
- prior actions;
- context.

No generic Charisma stat or random persuasion system conflicts with GDS-5 capability-based progression.

## 14. Environmental Storytelling — Result: PASS

Environmental evidence must match actual:
- damage;
- atmosphere;
- power;
- doors;
- station/ship systems.

It cannot contradict system simulation for visual drama.

## 15. Ending — Result: PASS

All three endings:
- stop immediate cascade;
- preserve home station;
- preserve postgame;
- create distinct strategic tradeoffs;
- avoid one hidden perfect option.

## 16. Postgame — Result: PASS

The finale records persistent world resolution state rather than resetting the save.

This is compatible with Global Save Philosophy.

## 17. Internal Narrative Consistency — Result: PASS

### Pillars vs Main Story
Competence, reconstruction, competing truth, and earned optimism are expressed in campaign structure.

### Main Story vs Characters
Each major character represents a distinct faction/ideological perspective.

### Main Story vs Lore
Evidence reveals the already-defined canon rather than inventing a different cause.

### Crew Storytelling vs Crew Mechanics
Narrative flavor does not add morale, romance, or random abandonment systems.

### Endgame vs Factions
Ending epilogues reflect faction/player relationship without forcing ownership changes.

## 18. Dependencies Preventing Design Complete

Narrative remains first-pass pending:

### GDS-8 Missions
- exact mission implementation;
- mission failure/retry;
- extraction and pacing.

### GDS-9 Combat
- combat encounters;
- boss/hostile behavior;
- ship/on-foot conflict.

### GDS-10 Robots
- narrative deployment of player/Continuance machines.

### GDS-11 Raids
- archive raids;
- boarding;
- finale sabotage/assault structure.

### GDS-12 Progression/Persistence
- exact coalition capability thresholds;
- world-state save semantics;
- endgame systemic rewards.

### GDS-13 Presentation
- cinematic scope;
- voice implementation;
- subtitle/comms UX;
- environmental storytelling presentation.

## 19. First-Pass Conclusion

No blocking contradiction was found.

Narrative can be marked:

**First-Pass Complete — Cross-Validation Pending**

GDS-8 can now design mission mechanics against a fixed campaign/world context instead of inventing narrative structure during implementation.
