# Galaxy Access Progression

> **Status:** Design Complete  
> **Authority:** Campaign access bands across Reach I–IV, route verification, shortcut rules, hard access gates, and alternate progression paths

## 1. Existing Authority

GDS-6 owns Strategic Reach capability.

GDS-7 owns the canonical location/route graph.

This document defines their progression relationship.

## 2. Core Rule

A player can use a strategic route only when all applicable conditions are satisfied:

- route discovered/known;
- route operational/verified where required;
- ship Reach >= route Reach requirement;
- navigation/propulsion configuration operational;
- story/faction/security access where the destination itself is restricted;
- no active route blocker that makes travel physically unavailable.

## 3. Reach Is Not Story Level

Completing an Act does not make an under-capable ship traverse a higher-Reach route.

Likewise, owning a Reach-capable ship does not automatically reveal unknown routes.

## 4. Reach I — Recovery Sector

Canonical route band:

- Aster's Rest ↔ Caldera;
- Aster's Rest ↔ Morrow;
- Caldera ↔ Morrow.

Primary purpose:

- establish Horizon;
- gather common resources;
- first survivors;
- restore local relay/navigation knowledge.

## 5. Reach II — Inner Frontier

Canonical route band includes:

- Morrow ↔ Vesper;
- Caldera ↔ Kara;
- Vesper ↔ Bastion;
- Vesper ↔ Orison;
- Kara ↔ Kestrel.

Unlock requires actual Reach II propulsion/navigation capability plus discovery/route conditions.

## 6. Reach III — Contested Reach / Outer Expanse

Canonical band includes:

- Bastion ↔ Lyra;
- Orison ↔ Nacre;
- Kestrel ↔ Nacre;
- Nacre ↔ Nadir;
- Lyra ↔ Pale Crown;
- Nadir ↔ Eidolon;
- Pale Crown ↔ Eidolon.

This band contains advanced resource, faction conflict, raid, fusion/quantum and Fracture-investigation content.

## 7. Reach IV — Fracture Verge

Canonical route:

- Eidolon ↔ Threshold;
- Threshold ↔ Null Choir;
- Null Choir ↔ Crownless.

Reach IV requires late Research/navigation/ship capability and story-derived route information.

## 8. Shortcut Routes

Existing GDS-7 optional shortcuts remain locked until their explicit exploration/restoration condition commits.

A shortcut cannot be purchased as a generic map unlock unless its authored route condition permits that source.

## 9. Route Discovery

A route can become known through:

- exploration;
- restored relay;
- faction data;
- recovered archive;
- mission/story output;
- purchased specific navigation intelligence.

Knowledge does not guarantee physical traversability.

## 10. Route Verification

Unverified/damaged routes can require:

- survey;
- beacon/reference placement;
- repair;
- anomaly calibration;
- specific navigation evidence.

Verification is persistent once legitimately committed unless an authored Dynamic Event later disrupts the physical route.

## 11. Faction Restrictions

A destination can require docking/transit authorization even when the route is physically reachable.

The player can resolve access through authored:

- reputation;
- permit;
- service mission;
- alternate route;
- hostile approach/raid where supported.

## 12. No Universal Faction Softlock

Ordinary reputation choices cannot permanently eliminate every route to mandatory campaign progression.

At least one authored reconciliation/alternate physical path remains.

## 13. Dynamic Route Events

Dynamic Events can temporarily:

- increase risk;
- disrupt traffic;
- block a local service;
- create restoration mission.

They cannot permanently erase the sole mandatory campaign route without a recovery path.

## 14. Threat vs Access

A route/location can be accessible yet extremely dangerous.

Threat rating warns; it does not automatically prevent travel unless an actual hard requirement is unsatisfied.

## 15. Difficulty

Difficulty does not lower Reach requirements or auto-open routes.

## 16. Ship Damage

A ship that loses required propulsion/navigation capability can temporarily lose route eligibility until repaired.

The route itself remains known.

## 17. Alternate Ships

The player may use any owned ship that satisfies the route's actual requirements.

A specific hull name is not required unless the mission/dock physically demands it.

## 18. Information Persistence

Discovered routes remain committed Knowledge Assets and are not lost through raids/crew injury/ship destruction.

## 19. Postgame

Ending choice can alter route availability/world consequences where GDS-7 defines it.

Postgame continues using the same Reach/route validation rules.

## 20. Explicit Non-Goals

No phase-only route teleport, level-gated galaxy map, seamless astronomical travel, difficulty-based Reach bypass, or permanent procedural campaign route deletion.

## 21. Tuneable Parameters

Travel cost/time/risk, route-event frequency, scan/verification work, and service access costs are tuneable.

## 22. Dependencies

Depends on GDS-6 Reach/Navigation, GDS-7 Canonical Region Map, GDS-8 Missions, Research Progression, Faction Reputation Integration, Dynamic Events, and Failure/Recovery.

## 23. Open Questions

None in galaxy access progression baseline.
