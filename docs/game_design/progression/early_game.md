# Early Game — P0/P1

> **Status:** Draft  
> **Authority:** Early-game pacing goals, capability order, anti-softlock resources, onboarding-to-autonomy transition, and P1 exit conditions

## 1. Scope

Early Game covers:

- P0 Recovery / Recommissioning;
- P1 Local Operator / Reach I.

Primary geography:

- Horizon Station / Aster's Rest;
- Caldera;
- Morrow.

## 2. Early-Game Goal

The player learns the game's systemic loop by personally doing work that later becomes delegated:

Diagnose need  
→ leave station  
→ acquire/repair/rescue  
→ return  
→ process/build/recruit  
→ improve station/ship  
→ automate solved repetition.

## 3. Starting Pressure

Horizon begins damaged but recoverable.

The opening can create shortages/faults, but must not require knowledge/resources the player has had no possible way to obtain.

## 4. Protected Recovery Inputs

The opening world must provide at least one recoverable source path for foundational materials required to restore:

- basic structural repair;
- pressure sealing;
- power distribution;
- simple electronics;
- docking/ship repair;
- life-support support.

These can come from starting storage, salvage, Caldera resources, or guaranteed authored recovery targets.

## 5. No Opening Economic Dependency

The player must be able to restore basic self-sufficiency without requiring a large Credit balance or high faction reputation.

Trade becomes flexibility, not a mandatory rescue from an impossible starting state.

## 6. Initial Manual Operations

The player should personally perform examples of:

- repair;
- resource transfer;
- fabrication;
- mining/salvage;
- mission preparation;
- ship docking/travel;
- first environmental hazard response.

This establishes meaning before automation hides repetition.

## 7. First Expeditions

Early missions emphasize:

- basic resources;
- salvage;
- relay repair/recon;
- survivor rescue;
- simple combat;
- safe extraction understanding.

Full-scale fortified raids are not expected before the player understands mission/extraction and robot basics.

## 8. First Crew

P1 introduces survivor recruitment so the first meaningful station workload can become supervised/automated.

The campaign should expose opportunities for engineering, agriculture/medical, and logistics/robotics coverage without guaranteeing one exact character build.

## 9. First Robots

P1 introduces Recon/Repair roles and later basic Assault capability.

The player learns:

- manufacturing;
- charging;
- physical deployment;
- TCC;
- recovery/destruction distinction.

## 10. Early Combat

Combat focuses on readable ballistic/basic weapon use, shields/armor fundamentals, cover, ammo, and extraction risk.

The player is not expected to own every energy/rail/heavy weapon class.

## 11. Early Station Defense

Horizon can have blind spots and minimal defenses.

Ordinary procedural severe raids must not be generated before the player has been introduced to defense systems and has a reasonable opportunity to establish at least minimal detection/response capability.

## 12. Dynamic-Event Protection

P0/tutorial states suppress ordinary high-severity procedural Horizon raids.

The first hostile station-defense event, if used for onboarding, is explicitly authored and bounded.

## 13. Early Economy

Markets/contact trade introduce:

- Credits;
- finite stock;
- buy/sell spread;
- basic refuel/repair supplies.

The early economy should not make selling starter-critical equipment a progression trap without clear warning.

## 14. Reach I

By P1 the player's ship must be able to perform repeatable Reach I travel after legitimate repairs/fitting.

## 15. P1 Exit Conditions

P1 exits when the campaign has committed the required local story outputs and the player has a viable path to Reach II.

At minimum the world/campaign state must support:

- stable Horizon core operation;
- repeatable mission launch/return;
- verified relevant Recovery Sector route network;
- outside faction/contact path;
- Research/Blueprint path toward Reach II;
- operational ship capable of current routes.

No generic XP threshold is part of the gate.

## 16. Expected, Not Mandatory, Maturity

A well-prepared player likely has by P1 exit:

- first recruited crew;
- basic farming/water support;
- basic automation;
- at least one robot support capability;
- improved suit/weapons;
- modest Credit/trade access.

Optional omissions do not block story if actual hard requirements are otherwise satisfied.

## 17. Anti-Grind

Common early materials must be available through several sensible paths so the player is not forced to repeat one mission endlessly.

## 18. Failure Recovery

P0/P1 failures prioritize teaching/recovery rather than severe cascading punishment.

Consumed resources still remain consumed and robots can still be destroyed once those mechanics are introduced.

## 19. Difficulty

Every Difficulty supports the same early progression and content.

## 20. Explicit Non-Goals

Early Game does not require full robot army, Frigate, mature raids, high-end quantum/anomaly research, high reputation, or repetitive XP farming.

## 21. Tuneable Parameters

Starting stocks, early mission rewards, first-event cadence, resource yields, enemy pressure, and build/research durations are tuneable.

## 22. Dependencies

Depends on Progression Model, GDS-2 through GDS-10, Economy, Dynamic Events, Failure/Recovery, and GDS-13 onboarding.

## 23. Open Questions

None in the early-game progression contract.
